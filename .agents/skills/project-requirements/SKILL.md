# Requirements and specification

Read docs/PROJECT.md, docs/PROGRESS.md and the relevant task plan. Distinguish the user's confirmed statements from proposals and open questions; the feature list and technology stack are not a specification. Identify actors, MVP boundaries, functional and nonfunctional requirements, business rules and testable acceptance criteria only as supported by evidence. Ask focused questions for material gaps; do not fill them with plausible defaults.

Maintain traceability from confirmed requirements to acceptance criteria and later tests. Mark unresolved decisions and verification as such. Do not enter a new phase without user confirmation. Apply .agents/workflows/task.md.
