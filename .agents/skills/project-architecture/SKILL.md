# Architecture and design

Read the relevant confirmed requirements, state, existing design and decisions. Identify missing requirements before choosing boundaries, persistence, authentication, authorization, transactions or error handling. Mark alternatives and tradeoffs as proposals, verify uncertain technology details with official documentation, and record approved decisions with evidence and consequences. Do not treat a prospective technology stack as an approved architecture or alter approved architecture without authorization.

Relate designs to requirements and verifiable tests; do not fabricate endpoints, schemas, versions or business rules. Apply .agents/workflows/task.md and docs/SECURITY.md.
